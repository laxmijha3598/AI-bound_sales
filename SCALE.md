To scale from 10 leads/week to 1,000, I would split the system into queues with explicit review gates: sourcing, enrichment, personalization QA, email delivery, LinkedIn tasks, reply classification, and scheduling. I would replace raw public search with approved data providers or first-party intent sources, add email verification and deliverability controls, rotate copy by segment, enforce daily send limits per mailbox, and keep LinkedIn human-in-the-loop unless using an approved partner API. The biggest engineering changes would be durable job storage, retry/idempotency keys, bounce handling, consent/suppression lists, domain warmup monitoring, and dashboards for reply rate, positive rate, meetings booked, and spam complaints.
